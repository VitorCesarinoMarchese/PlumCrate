Find your favorites.

Browse photo cards or a compact list. Search names and filenames, and switch individual mods on or off.

Bring your collection.

Import a folder or ZIP. Review existing game mods before moving them into your managed library. Your originals stay in place.

Read the app guide ↗

  1. Choose your game folder

    Point the app to the Sims 4 folder that contains Mods. You can change this later in Settings.

  2. Add your mods

    Open Add mods and import a folder or ZIP. Review the import. New imports start enabled.

  3. Make it yours

    Use a mod's switch to choose what loads. Open its details to inspect files and saved sources.

A few things to know.

Does it work on Windows or macOS?

Linux is the supported platform. Windows and macOS have not been verified.

Do I need a CurseForge API key?

Only for CurseForge browsing, browser-assisted installation, source lookup, and update checks. Save your own key in local Settings. Local mod management works without it.

Are mod updates automatic?

No. You initiate update checks for attached projects. Notices open the provider page; you download and replace local files yourself, then record the installed release.

Where are my files stored?

Your library and settings stay on your computer. Optional provider features and remote previews use the network. AppImages check GitHub for app updates, which you can turn off in Settings.
